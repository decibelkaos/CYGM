📋 What to Expect
  1. Plug in your device using a USB cable that can transfer data (some cables only charge).
  2. Choose your firmware version from the list above. We've already selected the newest one for you.
  3. Follow the button pressing steps when you click "Start Flashing". Don't worry - we'll guide you through it with clear timing.
  4. Wait about 1-2 minutes while your device updates. You'll see a progress bar. Please don't unplug the cable during this time.
  5. Done! Your device will turn back on automatically with the new software. You can then set up your WiFi and glucose monitor settings.
❓ Troubleshooting
First, make sure your USB cable can transfer data - some cables only charge phones. Try a different cable if you have one. Also try plugging it into a different USB port on your computer. If that doesn't work, you may need to install a small driver program. Your device uses a chip called CH340. Download the driver from wch-ic.com and install it, then restart your computer.
